Konstanz-area public household electricity and weather, 2016

Meter source: Open Power System Data, Household Data, release 2020-04-15.
Contributors: CoSSMic consortium and Adrian Minde (ISC Konstanz).
Source and processing: https://data.open-power-system-data.org/household_data/2020-04-15/
Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: Selected five residential grid-import channels. Converted consecutive
15-minute cumulative readings to half-hour kWh in the 2016 Europe/Berlin
calendar year. UTC start labels are used. publisher_interpolated=1 means the
publisher marked at least one of the three counter boundaries interpolated.

Weather source: Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S), ERA5 reanalysis,
retrieved using the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API for the Konstanz area
(latitude 47.6603, longitude 9.1758). This package contains modified Copernicus
Climate Change Service information. The European Commission and ECMWF are
not responsible for any use that may be made of this information.
Open-Meteo API: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api
ERA5 source: https://doi.org/10.24381/cds.adbb2d47
Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Changes: Selected temperature, precipitation, wind speed and horizontal
irradiance. Shifted preceding-hour precipitation and irradiance into UTC
start-labelled intervals. Weather represents a grid cell shared by all homes.

Methods and results:
https://github.com/charlie9578/domestic-energy-analysis/blob/main/research/OBSERVED_VALIDATION.md
