## Source hierarchy and comparisons

- Prefer **metered heat output + metered electricity** with defined system boundary for SPF; compare EoH annual-window SPFH2/SPFH3/SPFH4 with *equivalent* monitoring boundaries only. A manufacturer's SCOP design estimate is not achieved performance.
- Prefer **paired intervals of actual consumption and actual tariff validity spans** for bills. Ofgem's regional average quarter is a **benchmark scenario**, not a replacement for a household's actual historic contract.
- Prefer **NEED** for conditional typical property comparisons, **subnational** data for region totals and means, **LCL** for a historical half-hourly experiment, and **IDEAL** for joint gas/electric/temperature inference. Do not combine their populations without weighting or matching.
- Community monitoring and demonstration programmes are not representative random samples; report sampling, period, exclusions, uncertainty and meter inclusion alongside estimates.

## Licence and ethics policy

Government text/tables generally follow their listed Open Government Licence notices, but **check the licence on each download**. HeatpumpMonitor API material is shared publicly, but publicity does **not** waive the platform's terms or contributors' privacy. London/IDEAL/REFIT/UK-DALE/UKDS files have different release and attribution terms. Do not publish raw private smart-meter readings, coordinates, household identifiers, account keys, EPC-linked addresses, or a combined reidentification dataset. For external scientific reuse, cite original authors and persistent dataset identifiers; keep this repository's sample CSVs synthetic.
