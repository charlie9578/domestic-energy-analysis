What this catalogue is—and is not

These are published product claims and documented data-access routes, not independent product rankings or endorsements. A public dashboard is not automatically a public household-level dataset. Hardware, account access, licensing and prices change. No household account data is shared here.

Suggested independent evaluation

Start with HeatpumpMonitor, EoH, energy-stat tariff portals, Octopus Compare, Glow/Bright, Home Assistant/EMHASS/Predbat, and independently monitored heat-pump controls. Compare identical usage, weather, DST and pricing fixtures. Demand error bars, measured outcomes and realistic comfort constraints before claiming a technology wins.
