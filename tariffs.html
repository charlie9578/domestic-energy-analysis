Reprice the same load

On the observed test slots, illustrative 10p overnight and 30p daytime rates cost less for two homes than a 27p flat rate. All readings stayed fixed.

Shift a metered load

Across three eligible overnight EV windows, 13.15 kWh moved to cheaper slots and modelled energy charges fell by £2.63. Total charging energy and the observed half-hour peak were preserved. Vehicle availability through the deadline was assumed.

Reduce imported energy

A verified cut of 1 kWh in a 30p interval saves 30p. Moving it to a 10p interval saves 20p. The forecast shows where to look for savings. A separate measurement or engineering calculation establishes what can be removed.
