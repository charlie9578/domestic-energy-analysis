Hourly heat-balance validation

We modelled one monitored room as a single thermal zone. The model uses metered delivered heat, outdoor temperature and an effective allowance for unmeasured gains. It was fitted on 8–15 January and evaluated on 16–21 January 2024.

Persistence carries the first indoor reading forward. Each heat-balance run starts at the same reading and uses the heat that was actually delivered. The short test therefore assesses temperature prediction under the recorded heating, and provides no evidence about changing the thermostat.

The on-site model’s effective heat loss was 194 W/K, with a day-block bootstrap interval of 41–348 W/K. The estimate describes this fit and should not be used for equipment sizing.
